title: "Appendices"
---

The appendices hold reference material that supports the main text but would slow it down if left in place — extra terminology, formulas, tax mechanics, and one detailed implementation note.

```text
A1 Additional Terminology - covered calls, straddles, strangles, calendars, pin risk, gamma scalping
A2 Mathematical Formula - Black-Scholes pricing and the full Greeks derivative table
A3 Tax Considerations - Section 1256 treatment, wash sales, constructive sales, by instrument
A4 Crash Repricing Methodology - how V(crash) is computed in the hedge-sizing engine
```

A1–A3 are general reference, useful whenever a term or tax rule from the main text needs unpacking. A4 is different in kind: it documents the specific repricing engine behind the crash-scenario numbers used in Part VI and Part X, including the skew treatment and the policy keys that control it — read it when the *mechanics* behind a crash convexity figure matter, not just the definition.

**Tax content is for general orientation only** and should not substitute for advice from qualified tax counsel.
