---
title: "Monetization and Re-Risk Rules"
---

A tail hedge only does half its job by paying out in a crash. The other half is what happens to those gains afterward — whether they are realized, and whether they are redeployed while assets are still cheap.

This part covers the **monetization side** of a hedge program:

```text
1 why unmanaged hedge gains can disappear in a rebound
2 the accumulate -> hold -> monetize -> redeploy -> rebuild cycle
3 typical triggers for monetizing (volatility spikes, drawdown levels, hedge value)
4 the tension between taking profits and holding for convexity
5 rules for re-risking and rebuilding protection after markets stabilize
6 a scenario-based playbook mapping market moves to hedge and portfolio actions
7 practical execution guidance for unwinding hedges during a live crisis
```

Together these sections turn "the hedge paid off" into a repeatable process: locking in liquidity, deploying it into discounted assets, and resetting the program for the next cycle.
