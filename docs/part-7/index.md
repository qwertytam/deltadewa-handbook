---
title: "Designing a Tail-Hedge Program"
---

Designing a systematic tail-hedge program involves decisions across seven dimensions:

- governance and constraints,
- sizing,
- convexity and premium budgeting,
- strike selection,
- maturity selection,
- rolling rules, and
- ongoing evaluation

Those seven cover designing a program.
[Continuity Planning](continuity-planning.md) covers what must be written down
before the person running it cannot.
