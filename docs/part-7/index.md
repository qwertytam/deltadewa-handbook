---
title: "Designing a Tail-Hedge Program"
---

Designing a systematic tail-hedge program involves decisions across seven dimensions:

- governance and constraints,
- sizing,
- convexity and premium budgeting,
- strike selection,
- maturity selection,
- rolling rules, and
- ongoing evaluation

Those seven cover designing a program. Two further pages cover running one:
[Weekly Review Cadence](weekly-review-cadence.md) for the operating rhythm once
the program is live, and [Continuity Planning](continuity-planning.md) for what
must be written down before the person running it cannot.
