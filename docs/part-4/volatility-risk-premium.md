---
title: "Volatility Risk Premium"
---

Markets tend to price **implied volatility higher than realized volatility**.[^hist-put-writing]

Formally:

\$VRP = IV - RV$

Where:

- \$IV$ = implied volatility
- \$RV$ = realized volatility

*Example:*

```text
IV = 22%
RV = 18%
```

Premium:

```text
4%
```

Option sellers capture this on average.
