title: "Educational Resources"
---

This part is a curated set of further reading and resources for going deeper on volatility and tail-risk hedging than a single handbook can cover.

It is organized by format:

```text
Books - practitioner and academic texts on vol surfaces, skew, and convexity
Research papers - institutional and academic research on tail-risk hedging
Online courses - structured introductions to options and derivatives
Youtube - practitioner commentary on dealer positioning and volatility regimes
Websites - ongoing sources of volatility and options market data
```

None of this is required to use the rest of the handbook. It is here for readers who want to build intuition beyond the definitions and frameworks covered elsewhere, or who want primary sources for the concepts referenced throughout.
