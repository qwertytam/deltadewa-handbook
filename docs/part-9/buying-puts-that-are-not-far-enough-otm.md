---
title: "Buying Puts That Are Not Far Enough OTM"
---

Investors often buy ATM or slightly OTM puts.

Example:

```text
SPX = 5000
Put strike = 4700
```

These options have:

- high theta
- moderate convexity
- weaker skew exposure

#### Why Funds Avoid This

```text
deep OTM puts reprice dramatically
```

Example (March 2020):

| Strike      | Price change |
| ----------- | ------------ |
| ATM put     | ~5×          |
| 30% OTM put | ~30×         |

Deep OTM options benefit from:

```text
price drop
+ volatility spike
+ skew steepening
```
